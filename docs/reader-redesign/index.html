Three directions for the reader header

All three fix the same root causes you named: the title floating in a centered void, the Arabic/English type reading as two unrelated systems, and Catalog/title/search styled in isolation. The search field stays in place in every direction — the title and Catalog are what move to harmonize with it. The body (which you liked) is untouched.

Direction A
Balanced bilingual masthead

Title becomes a true centered masthead; English is promoted from a light caption to a tracked, gold-ruled companion with matched optical weight. Catalog and search become symmetric flanks of equal weight.
