# Council submission — editorial continuity lens (2026-07-02)

The library used the box as its default container while the landing reserves
boxes for editorial moments; when everything is a card, the alive treatment
loses meaning. The rotating hero was a slideshow, not a shelf: 1 of 18
highlights visible, on a timer, over an invented codex-cover artifact. The
segmented control was bare mechanism with no bilingual pairing.

Prescription: the shelf as open composition (4-6 bilingual lockups over one
gold hairline reading as the shelf, thin hairlines as book edges), headed by
an apparatus rule with mono marginalia; domain and category cards become
fihrist rows in the landing's grammar; era heads become apparatus rules over
OPEN compact rows (no click tax); one-line work rows with dotted leaders and
a consistent mono margin column; volumes demoted to a sort and a meta
column. Alive cards only where genuine curation exists, otherwise nowhere.

Anchors: Klim's fonts index (each row a specimen of itself: the Arabic label
IS the row's display moment), the Morgan Library's manuscript pages (objects
on open ground with apparatus captions), Arion Press's backlist (mono
marginalia as archival gravitas).
