# Council submission — catalog IA lens (2026-07-02)

Closed accordions destroy scent; a list you open one drawer at a time is a
filing cabinet, not a browse. "By volumes" is a sort pretending to be a view.
Author is a filter and sort key in this corpus, not a grouping dimension (417
distinct authors in a 600-work sample; the most prolific held 10). No
position sense; the 600 cap silently truncated five categories (up to 42% of
a scope) and every domain, violating wrong-is-worse-than-absent.

Recommended, ranked: (1) one list, combinable facets, explicit sort; kill
the exclusive views; (2) the era spread as clickable counts, undated as a
first-class labeled bucket; (3) compact ~44px rows plus load-more in pages
of 100 with a persistent "Showing X of Y"; (4) remove "Begin here", fold
canonical rank into the list as a badge, make the hero a static shelf of 4-6
visible works. Wayfinding: sticky scope header with total and removable
filter chips; per-group counts everywhere.

References: al-Maktaba al-Shamela (counts at every tree node), Discogs
(count-annotated combinable facets), Archive.org (clickable date histogram),
IMDb advanced search (position line; sort-not-view framing).
