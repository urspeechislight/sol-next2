# Council submission — browsing journey lens (2026-07-02)

Intent map: known-item lookup needs typeahead from anywhere, not a
domain-guess walk; domain orientation needs the landscape visible together
(categories with counts, era spread, landmarks), which a one-at-a-time
carousel hides; guided entry was served by "Begin here"'s instinct under a
tutorial label, and its job moves to a claimed shelf; completionist scanning
wants density and open lists with both AH and CE labels. "By volumes" maps
to no real intent: nobody arrives wanting large books; retire the view, keep
the signal as metadata and one ordering.

Engagement, ranked: recognizable landmarks first (seeing al-Kāfī tells the
reader the catalog knows its tradition), scent second (counts and names
before any click), one-tap resumability third, narrative framing last.

The shelf: label it أمهات الكتب, "Foundational works": a scholarly claim
backed by the canonical-rank data, not app promotion. Static, all visible,
one line of credential per work, no motion. Domain rooms: one per major
category so the shelf doubles as a map. Tap-through opens at the first
content chapter with the TOC visible, not page-1 front matter.

First-minute test: tapping Fiqh should state size, divisions, and which
books matter within ten seconds, no motion, no wall of closed drawers.

References: Daunt Books shelf-talkers (one line of why), Criterion Channel
editorial rows (claimed rows that hold still), the Rijksmuseum highlights
(a small canon deep-linking into the full filtered catalog).
