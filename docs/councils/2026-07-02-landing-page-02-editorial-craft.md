# Council submission — editorial / brand craft lens (2026-07-02)

## Diagnosis

No focal point: four near-equal translucent cards violate the first law of
composition; a 2×2 of equals is a menu, not a page. No narrative arc: the
page is all middle, and unbounded trailing emptiness reads as an unfinished
export, not restraint. Arabic never speaks at display scale even though it is
the product's greatest typographic asset; the ayah sits at body size inside a
card. The corpus is invisible: authority unstated is authority absent. The
ornament is timid: a faint rosette half off-screen registers as a rendering
artifact. A centered 900px column is the assembled-not-designed tell.

## Prescription

The organizing metaphor is a manuscript page: a dominant matn block with a
narrower hāshiya margin, roughly 62/38. The hero is the ayah, set large in
calligraphic display type, right-aligned as Arabic demands, translation
beneath at a fraction of that scale. Above it one committed ornament: a
full-width illuminated ʿunwān rendered as flat gold SVG. The Hijri date
becomes an editorial dateline. The margin column stacks the hadith (grading,
parallels) and the featured book with its rationale; chronicle and calendar
become compact marginalia. The close is a fihrist: the taxonomy typeset as a
contents band with true counts inside a working index, never stat blocks.
Color strategy Committed: paper is the field, ink the voice, gold graduates
from accent to structure. Motion: one orchestrated load under 1.2 seconds,
then stillness.

## Anchor references (technique catalogs only; re-render in paper-and-gold)

- Sultan Baybars Qurʾān frontispiece (British Library Add. MS 22406):
  carpet-page grammar, geometry framing the text panel.
- Klim Type Foundry specimens: type-as-image; one word so large it becomes
  the picture, applied to the Arabic.
- Aga Khan Museum collection pages: object staging; one artifact on a
  generous field with a disciplined caption block.

The test for every choice: would this feel at home as the opening page of an
illuminated codex that happens to be interactive?
